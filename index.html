Connect Spotify

  1. Open the Spotify dashboard

    Log in at developer.spotify.com/dashboard. The account that owns the app needs Spotify Premium. Development apps are limited to one per developer.

    Open dashboard
  2. Create the app

    Tap Create app. Any name and description work. Under “Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?” tick Web API only, accept the terms, then Save.

    In Redirect URIs add exactly this, then tap Add:

    It must match character for character, including the trailing slash. Spotify accepts https:// or http://127.0.0.1:PORT/, never localhost.

  3. Add your Spotify account

    Open your app, then Settings → User Management. Add the name and email of the Spotify account you will log in with. In development mode, only listed users can connect, and Spotify caps how many. Skipping this is the most common cause of “forbidden”.

  4. Copy the Client ID

    In Settings → Basic Information, copy the Client ID. You do not need the Client secret.

  5. Paste it and connect
    Redirect URI (advanced)
  6. Accept every permission

    On Spotify’s screen, tap Agree. Declining any of these breaks a feature:

    • user-top-readReads your top artists and tracks to seed your taste.
    • user-library-modifySaves songs to your Liked Songs.
    • playlist-read-privateReads your private playlists for import.
    • playlist-read-collaborativeReads shared playlists for import.
    • playlist-modify-privateCreates private playlists from Moods.
Something went wrong?

“Forbidden” or 403: your account is not under User Management, the app owner is not on Premium, or you connected before a permission was added. Fix that, tap Disconnect, then Connect and accept everything.

INVALID_CLIENT or redirect error: the Redirect URI in the dashboard does not match the one above exactly.

Playlist import fails: Spotify only returns tracks for playlists you own or collaborate on. Copy the playlist into your own first.

Opened as a file or localhost: serve the folder with python3 -m http.server 8000 and open http://127.0.0.1:8000/.
